🛰️ WIFI ODDS

Choose where WiFi Odds can help.

United and Navan work immediately. Alaska and Google Flights are optional: grant only the sites you use, from this page or later in the extension popup.

  • united.com

    access on

    Per-flight next-gen odds and labels. Single-airline results sort automatically; undo and Settings remain available.

  • alaskaair.com

    optional access off

    Per-flight next-gen odds and labels. Single-airline results sort automatically; undo and Settings remain available.

  • app.navan.com

    access on

    United rows can show per-flight odds. The booking site's order is preserved until you choose Prioritize United.

  • Google Flights

    optional access off

    Recognized airlines get ConnectScore; a visible United or Alaska flight number can upgrade to per-flight odds. WiFi Odds never reorders Google Flights.

    Chrome grants all of www.google.com. WiFi Odds injects only on google.com/travel/flights search results—not Search, Gmail, or checkout.
